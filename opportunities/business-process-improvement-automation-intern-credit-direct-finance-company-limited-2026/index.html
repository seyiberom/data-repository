Role overview

An internship focused on business process improvement and automation. The intern will help document and improve workflows, support automation efforts, and contribute to testing, reporting, and change adoption activities.

Responsibilities

  • Assist in documenting current (As-Is) business processes across departments
  • Support the design of improved (To-Be) process flows and workflows
  • Identify inefficiencies, bottlenecks, and opportunities for automation
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to gather requirements and validate process changes
  • Support the implementation of automation solutions
  • Participate in testing of new processes, systems, or enhancements
  • Maintain process documentation, SOPs, change logs, and training materials
  • Track process performance metrics and support reporting
  • Assist in change management and user adoption activities
  • Develop dashboards and reports to track KPIs and measure impact
  • Assist in running pilot tests and gathering feedback before rollouts
  • Present findings and recommendations to team leads and departments

Required qualifications

  • Must have strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Good understanding of process mapping (e.g. flowcharts, BPMN)
  • Familiarity with tools such as Excel, Visio, Whimsical, Lucidchart, or similar
  • Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills
  • Detail-oriented with strong organizational abilities
  • Location eligibility: Nigeria
