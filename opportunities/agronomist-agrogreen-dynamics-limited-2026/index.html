Role overview

Internship role focused on agronomy support, crop monitoring, soil testing, pest and disease scouting, and farm data/reporting. The role also includes farmer training, demonstrations, and support for field school activities.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct daily crop monitoring and record plant growth and pest/disease signs
  • Perform soil health testing (basal, mid-season, final assessments)
  • Document all farm inputs and maintain input records (fertilizer, seeds, pesticides)
  • Scout for pests and diseases; prepare weekly pest/disease reports
  • Coordinate and assist with BioSAT demonstrations for farmers
  • Collect climate and yield data for CSV-P baseline reporting
  • Prepare monthly technical reports and data summaries
  • Train and guide farm workers on climate-smart practices
  • Support farmer field schools (FFS) activities and demonstration sessions

Required qualifications

  • Currently a student or recent graduate
  • ND/HND/BSc in Agronomy, Crop Science, Plant Science, or Agricultural Technology
  • Strong interest in climate-smart agriculture and sustainable farming
  • Good writing skills
  • Basic computer skills (MS Excel, WhatsApp, email)
  • Communication skills
  • Committed to hands-on farm work

Required skills

  • Crop monitoring

  • Record keeping
  • Report writing
  • Data collection
  • Training and guidance of farm workers
  • Basic computer use (MS Excel, WhatsApp, email)
  • Communication and presentation to farmers

Preferred skills

  • Local knowledge of Edo State

Experience level

Currently a student or recent graduate Internship

Who should consider applying

Students or recent graduates with ND/HND/BSc training in agronomy-related fields who are interested in climate-smart agriculture, comfortable with hands-on farm work, and able to write reports and use basic computer tools.

Skills to strengthen for future opportunities

If you are preparing for this kind of role, focus on building confidence in farm-based observation, clear report writing, basic Excel/data entry, and communicating practical guidance to farmers.
