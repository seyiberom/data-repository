About the organisation

Open Access Consulting is described as a professional services firm offering strategic solutions in human capital development, expatriate and immigration services, recruitment, background check services, value-added consulting, and outsourcing services.

Role overview

This internship supports day-to-day business and HR operations at Open Access Consulting. The role offers practical exposure to HR and consulting work across recruitment, client support, training, and general operations.

Responsibilities

  • Support recruitment activities including candidate sourcing, CV screening, interview scheduling and follow-up.
  • Assist with employee/candidate background checks and verification assignments.
  • Support social media management, content development and digital marketing activities.
  • Assist with identifying prospective clients and supporting business development and client acquisition.
  • Support the planning, coordination and delivery of training programmes.
  • Assist with preparing proposals, presentations, reports and other client documents.
  • Maintain accurate recruitment, client, candidate and business development records.
  • Conduct research and provide administrative and operational support to the consulting team.
  • Communicate professionally with candidates, clients, vendors and other stakeholders.
  • Perform other related duties assigned by team lead to support the company's operations and growth.

Required qualifications

  • Bachelor's Degree/HND in Human Resources, Business Administration, Management, Marketing, Mass Communication, Psychology, Social Sciences or a related discipline.
  • Must be a serving NYSC Corps Member (26B stream 1 & 2).
  • Good knowledge of Microsoft Office/Google Workspace.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills.
  • Candidates who reside in or around Ikeja, Lagos will be given preference.
