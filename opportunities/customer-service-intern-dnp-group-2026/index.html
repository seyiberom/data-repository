Role overview

DNP GROUP is seeking a Customer Service Intern to support customer interactions and office operations. The role involves handling enquiries, follow-ups, records, and administrative support.

Responsibilities

  • Attend to customer enquiries through phone, WhatsApp, email, and other channels
  • Respond professionally to customer requests and complaints
  • Schedule and follow up on customer appointments and service requests
  • Maintain accurate customer records and update databases
  • Follow up with customers after services to ensure satisfaction
  • Support the team with administrative and customer service tasks
  • Communicate customer feedback to the relevant team members
  • Assist with preparing reports and maintaining service records

Required qualifications

  • HND, B.Sc., or equivalent qualification in any relevant field
  • NYSC members and other interns are encouraged to apply
  • Good verbal and written communication skills
  • Customer-focused and friendly personality
  • Basic computer and Microsoft Office skills
  • Ability to use WhatsApp and other communication platforms professionally
  • Good organizational and time-management skills
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
