About the organisation

The British Council is the UK’s international organisation for cultural relations and educational opportunities. It is a UK charity governed by Royal Charter and a UK public body, working across arts and culture, English language, education and civil society.

Role overview

This internship offers students and graduates practical experience in facilities management. The intern will support day-to-day facilities operations, maintenance coordination and related administrative and logistical tasks.

Responsibilities

  • Understudy and learn Facilities processes, leadership, stakeholder and vendor management through interactions with Facilities Staff in location
  • Assist the Facilities Team in-country in managing the Port Harcourt facilities and estate effectively and efficiently
  • Support proactive maintenance management by undertaking daily checks of facilities and equipment
  • Help maintain the office and estate by supporting security arrangements and customer care services
  • Support day-to-day coordination of facilities services and project activities and submit relevant reports
  • Assist with coordination of facilities and building improvement activities and liaise with stakeholders
  • Provide logistical support to visitors coming to Port Harcourt
  • Support planning and management of vehicles and drivers for transport support
  • Provide basic administrative support for IT-related activities as required

Required qualifications

  • Graduate-level education in any field
  • Graduated within the last four years

Required skills

  • Learning from and contributing to the workplace
  • Facilities process awareness
  • Stakeholder management
  • Vendor management
  • Maintenance monitoring
  • Report submission
  • Liaising with internal and external stakeholders
  • Logistical support
  • Basic administrative support for IT-related activities

Experience level

Graduate-level internship Graduated within the last four years

Who should consider applying

Students and graduates with graduate-level education in any field who finished within the last four years and want practical experience in facilities management.

Skills to strengthen for future opportunities

If you are preparing an application, focus on showing how you can support facilities operations, work with stakeholders and vendors, keep accurate reports, and handle coordination and administrative tasks in a professional setting.
